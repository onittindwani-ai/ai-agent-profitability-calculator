# Limits and honesty

Please read this before you make a decision with these numbers.

## It is an estimate

The calculator is only as good as the numbers you give it. Token counts change from run to run. Bills arrive late. Human time is easy
to guess too low. Treat the result as a careful estimate, and check it against your real bills every month.

## The prices are example rates

The token prices in the samples ($3 per 1M input tokens and $15 per 1M output tokens) are example rates. They are not the price of any
real model. Replace them with your provider's current price per 1M tokens. Prices change often.

## The sample data is fictional

The 3 sample workflows, their clients and their numbers are made up to show how the tool works. They are not real results and not
real clients.

## Not financial advice

This is a free tool to help you think about your costs. It is not financial, tax or legal advice. For big decisions, talk to an
accountant or a qualified advisor.

## Some context

AI agent costs are a real topic for large companies too. Reuters (Tatiana Bautzer, 10 November 2026) reported that McKinsey's Global
Banking Annual Review projects token spending at banks "could easily reach 20% of total labor costs". That is a projection for banks,
not a measured result, and it may not apply to your business. Source:
https://www.zawya.com/en/world/banks-seek-to-rein-in-soaring-ai-agent-costs-mckinsey-says-2169978

## What it does not do

- It does not connect to your model provider or read your bills. You type the numbers in.
- It does not know your taxes, payment fees or overhead outside the workflow.
- Gross margin here means revenue minus the cost to deliver this workflow. It is not your company's net profit.
